Route Comparison — Process Metrics vs. Supply Geography

Route economics and route supply resilience can tell different stories. Facts only: there is no combined score and no recommended route.

What do these numbers mean?
  • Assessed reagent spend is the cost of the reagents sent to this audit (production scale from the Planner). Solvents and other route costs are not assessed, so percentages are of reagent spend, not of total route cost.
  • Concentration uses supplier-country shares from trade data (USITC: share of U.S. import value; WITS: share among the top listed exporters). U.S. import origins are not global supply and exclude U.S. domestic production.
  • Largest single-country exposure = Σ (reagent spend × that country's share) for the country with the highest total.
  • Geographic (country conditions) = 100 − World Bank WGI political-stability governance score of the dominant origin. It is a governance indicator, not a disruption probability, and is reported separately from concentration.
  • Reagents without trade data are counted as unknown, never as safe or as high risk.

Detailed Risk Report

Origin / Stability describe country conditions for the dominant origin. Concentration describes how concentrated the reported supply is, with its source. These are reported separately.
